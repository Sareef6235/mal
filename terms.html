Terms & Conditions

These terms define acceptable use of the AstraEdu educational and admin management interface for secure login, receipts, plans, reports, quiz verification, and PWA workflows.

1. Platform Use

Users must operate the platform only for authorized educational administration, class planning, finance review, receipt approval, quiz review, and reporting tasks.

2. Security Duties

Administrators and staff are responsible for protecting credentials, respecting OTP verification flows, avoiding shared sessions, and reporting suspicious access prompts immediately.

3. Finance Records

Receipt, payment, and monthly plan data must be entered accurately. Approval, rejection, trash restore, and permanent deletion actions should follow institutional policy.

4. Exports & Printing

PDF, print, and spreadsheet exports may contain sensitive learner or finance information and should only be distributed to permitted recipients.

5. Availability

PWA offline support improves continuity, but final synchronization and official reporting depend on approved backend services and network availability.
